Typography

Dada typography is deliberate chaos. Fonts collide, sizes clash, orientations shift. Every word is a cut-out pasted at an angle. The typewriter, the broadsheet headline, the rubber stamp -- all coexist in defiant disharmony.

06

Cards & Panels

Content containers as collage fragments -- each panel a scrap of meaning torn from context and reassembled. Slight rotations and rough borders evoke the hand-assembled broadsheet.

The Readymade
Duchamp's urinal, signed "R. Mutt," entered the exhibition and shattered every assumption about what art could be. The object was not made -- it was chosen.
Marcel Duchamp -- Fountain, 1917
Sound Poetry
"gadji beri bimba glandridi lauli lonni cadori" -- Hugo Ball stood on stage in a cardboard costume, chanting nonsense syllables that stripped language to its primal vibration.
Hugo Ball -- Karawane, 1916
Photomontage
Hannah Hoch sliced through magazines, recombining heads, bodies, and machinery into visions of a fragmented modernity that no single photograph could capture.
Hannah Hoch -- Cut with the Kitchen Knife, 1919
Merzbau
Schwitters built cathedrals from garbage -- bus tickets, wire, newspaper, broken wood. Merz was everything Dada was and more: total art from total refuse.
Kurt Schwitters -- Merzbau, 1923-1937
The Manifesto
"DADA DOES NOT MEAN ANYTHING." Tzara's manifestos were performances in themselves -- contradictory, explosive, designed to destroy the very notion of a manifesto.
Tristan Tzara -- Dada Manifesto, 1918
Chance Operations
Jean Arp tore paper into fragments and let them fall where they may. The composition was not designed -- it was surrendered to the laws of randomness.
Jean Arp -- According to the Laws of Chance, 1916
07

Alerts

Notifications as communiques, bulletins, and declarations. Each alert carries the urgency of the Dada broadsheet -- information delivered with the weight of a rubber stamp.

The next Cabaret Voltaire performance has been scheduled for the 14th. Attendance is compulsory. Bring scissors.
Your submission to the Salon des Independants has been received. No jury. No prizes. No returns.
The bourgeoisie have been alerted to our activities. Proceed with maximum absurdity.
We declare that the automobile, which has accelerated our static age, cannot cure the stupidity of mankind. DADA alone does not smell.
DADA is against the future. DADA is dead. DADA is absurd. Long live DADA. We are not responsible for anything.

Manifesto Panel

The dark proclamation block -- for when content must be delivered as a declaration against reason itself.

A Declaration
Every product of disgust capable of becoming a negation of the family is Dada; protest with the fists of its whole being engaged in destructive action: Dada; knowledge of all the means rejected up until now by the shamefaced sex of comfortable compromise and good manners: Dada; abolition of logic, which is the dance of those impotent to create.
