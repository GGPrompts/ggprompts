Health Systems (VHA)

The Veterans Health Administration operates one of the nation's largest healthcare systems. VistA has been the backbone for 30+ years, with Oracle Health (Millennium) gradually replacing it under the EHRM program.

VistA Ecosystem

VistA consists of 180+ applications running across ~130 instances at VA medical centers. Built on M (MUMPS) with the FileMan database engine and CPRS as the primary clinician GUI.

EHRM Status

After a pause in 2023 due to user satisfaction concerns (79% of users disagreed the system enabled quality care), VA resumed planning in late 2024. FY2026 budget: $3.5B (+164% from FY2025). 13 facilities planned for 2026, 27 for 2027.

My HealtheVet (MHV)

The veteran-facing patient portal providing secure messaging, prescription refills, health records, and appointment scheduling. Currently migrating to va.gov (MHV-on-va.gov initiative).

Master Person Index

MPI/MVI is the single authoritative identity source across all VA. It cross-references identities across VistA, VBMS, DoD, and community records using the Integration Control Number (ICN) as the unique veteran identifier.
