How to Play: Move one tile per beat through procedural dungeons. Moving on the beat builds your combo multiplier. Moving off-beat breaks your combo. Enemies move on each beat too -- anticipate their patterns! Find the stairs > to descend deeper. Collect gold $ and weapon drops along the way. Survive until the song ends to win.
