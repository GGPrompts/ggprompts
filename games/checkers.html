Rules of the Realm

Thou art Red and movest first. Pieces advance diagonally. Captures art mandatory. Chain multiple jumps when able. Reach the far rank to be crowned King, who moveth in all diagonal directions. The sovereign who captures all enemy pieces, or traps them without move, claimeth victory.
