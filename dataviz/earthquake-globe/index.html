About this visualization — Earthquake data represents significant events (magnitude 6.0+) from 1960–2024, based on USGS Earthquake Catalog. Tectonic plate boundaries show where quakes cluster—the Ring of Fire, Mid-Atlantic Ridge, and Alpine-Himalayan Belt. Orthographic projection with hidden-hemisphere culling renders only the visible face of the Earth.
