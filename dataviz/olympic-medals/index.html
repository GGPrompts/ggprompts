About this visualization — Cumulative medal data is approximate, compiled from Wikipedia's all-time Olympic medal table and IOC records. The bubble chart plots total medals on X, gold ratio (gold/total) on Y, with bubble size proportional to total medals. Bar race mode shows the top 15 countries by cumulative total medals.
