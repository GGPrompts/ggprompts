Anthropic “Mythos” Model Leak Reveals Unprecedented Cyber Capabilities

A configuration error exposed internal documents describing a secret model codenamed “Capybara” that “far outpaces the efforts of defenders” — triggering a cybersecurity stock plunge.

Draft documents described the model as “a step change” beyond the Opus tier and “currently far ahead of any other AI model in cyber capabilities.”

Internal safety assessments warned the model could “exploit vulnerabilities in ways that far outpace the efforts of defenders,” raising alarms across the cybersecurity industry. Anthropic has not confirmed or denied the existence of the Mythos project, issuing only a brief statement acknowledging “an inadvertent exposure of pre-publication materials” and noting that “draft documents do not necessarily reflect shipping products or final capabilities.”

Federal Judge Blocks Pentagon’s Anthropic Blacklisting as “First Amendment Retaliation”

A 43-page preliminary injunction halts the Trump administration’s designation of Anthropic as a national security supply chain risk.

U.S. District Judge Rita Lin issued a 43-page preliminary injunction blocking the Trump administration’s designation of Anthropic as a national security “supply chain risk.” The ruling found that the government’s action was a direct response to Anthropic’s public refusal to grant the Department of Defense unfettered access to its Claude models for autonomous weapons and mass surveillance applications.

Judge Lin wrote that “punishing Anthropic for bringing public scrutiny to the government’s contracting position is classic illegal First Amendment retaliation,” adding that the company had demonstrated a likelihood of irreparable harm. The DOD had argued that restricting Anthropic from federal contracts was a routine procurement decision, but the court found the timing and internal communications revealed retaliatory intent.

The dispute originated when the Pentagon demanded unrestricted access to Claude’s capabilities for classified defense programs. Anthropic refused, citing its responsible scaling policy and concerns about autonomous weapons systems, then publicly disclosed the nature of the government’s demands — a move that drew bipartisan attention on Capitol Hill.

Gemini Embedding 2 Unifies All Modalities

Google launched Gemini Embedding 2, mapping text, images, video, audio, and PDFs into a single 3,072-dimensional vector space across 100+ languages — the first embedding model built natively on the Gemini architecture. The model tops the MTEB English leaderboard with a score of 68.32, a +5.81 margin over the previous leader.

Early adopters report a 70% latency reduction and 20% recall improvement over multi-model embedding pipelines, as applications no longer need separate encoders for each modality. The unified space enables cross-modal retrieval — searching for images using audio queries, or finding relevant video segments from text descriptions — without alignment layers.
